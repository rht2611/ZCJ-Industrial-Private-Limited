ZERO DEFECT PHILOSOPHY

Rigorous Quality Assurance & Metrology

Quality is engineered into every stage of production at ZCJ Industrial—starting from strict incoming raw material wire rod inspection, metallographic grain structure analysis, in-process SPC monitoring, to final 100% optical sorting.

  • Optical Profile Projector: Verifies thread pitch, tooth radius, and countersunk angles.
  • Micro-Vickers & Rockwell Hardness: Ensures core and case-hardened depth specifications.
  • Universal Tensile Testing: Validates proof load and breaking torque resistance.
  • Neutral Salt Spray Testing: Verifies corrosion durability per ASTM B117 standard.
